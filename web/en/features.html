S-01

A smart music library

Route diagram, scale 1:1
from a messy folder to a tidy catalogue

Drop your music into the music/ folder and the bot scans it and builds a library: it reads each file's tags (title, artist, album, genre, year, cover art). Files with missing tags get their names from the file and folder names, so nothing is left out.

Type a song, artist or album into /local and suggestions appear as you type. Want the whole set? Play a full album or an entire artist.

/local
Smart search with suggestions as you type, then plays exactly the track you pick.
/album
Plays a whole album in track order.
/artist
Plays the tracks of one artist in the library.
/favorites
Your own list of favourites: add, remove, list, play.
/nhaajt
Smart shuffle: never the same artist back to back, and it favours tracks the whole server likes.

add new files, no bot restart needed

S-02

Detail A: the Now Playing card

Enlarged 4:1, layer by layer
hover over it or press Explode drawing

The Now Playing card is rebuilt with Discord Components V2: cover art, an accent colour that changes with each track, a live progress bar while the music plays, and two rows of buttons right underneath. When the next track starts, the old card removes its buttons, so no dead buttons are left behind.

  1. 1Pause, resume. Press once to pause, again to carry on.
  2. 2Skip. With three or more listeners it becomes a vote: half of them must agree before the track changes. The person who requested it and DJs skip instantly.
  3. 3Stop. Stops playback and clears the queue.
  4. 4Loop. Each press cycles through: off, repeat track, repeat the whole queue.
  5. 5Shuffle. Reorders the tracks in the queue.
  6. 6Like (👍). Scores the track; liked tracks come up more often in /nhaajt.
  7. 7Dislike (👎). The opposite: the track is picked less often.
  8. 8Favourite (❤). Adds or removes the track from your /favorites.
  9. 9Lyrics (📜). Opens the synced lyrics, see sheet S-07.

/queue uses the same layout: cover art, title and page buttons. The controls only work when you are in the same voice channel as the bot; on servers with a DJ role, only DJs can press them.

S-03

The queue, seen in elevation

Main elevation, scale 1:20
the playing track sits on the ground floor

Add as many tracks as you like; each plays in its turn. /queue splits the queue into pages of ten, with cover art and page buttons in the new card layout.

Need to tidy up? /remove drops a track, /shuffle mixes the queue, /loop repeats the whole queue, /seek jumps to the best part, and /volume goes from 1 to 150.

Not sure of the exact title? /search looks things up by keyword (YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud or Spotify) and shows up to 10 results as numbered buttons: press a number and that track joins the queue.

Want to change the sound? /filter effect toggles an effect on or off for what is playing (bass boost, nightcore, vaporwave, 8D, karaoke, tremolo, vibrato, mono), and effects stack. /filter speed changes tempo from 0.5 to 2x with the pitch unchanged, /filter pitch changes pitch with the speed unchanged, and /filter eq sets bass, mid and treble from -2 to 10. /filter status shows what is applied and /filter reset clears everything.

Two new things to keep the peace: a fair queue, and /bump.

S-04

The bottomless loop: /nhaajt

Dial plan, scale 1:1
one shuffle per lap

Type /nhaajt when you want to hear the whole music folder without choosing track by track.

Every time you run it, the order is freshly shuffled. Each lap plays every track exactly once. When the lap ends, the bot reshuffles and carries on, and the first track of the new lap is never the one that just played. The music keeps going until you type /stop or /leave.

Since version 2.0 the shuffle has better taste: the bot avoids playing the same artist back to back, gives 👍 tracks more chances and 👎 tracks fewer. The 👍 and 👎 buttons on the Now Playing card are those votes, and they also feed the server's "most loved" board.

checked: no track gets left behind

The music folder lives on the machine running the bot, so this command plays the host's music.

Try the smart pick

Press 👍 or 👎 to change the weights, then draw the next track. Tracks by the same artist as the one that just played are excluded.

S-05

Music quiz: an exam hall in your server

Questions come from the server's own library
the faster you answer, the more you score

/quiz start opens a blind-test round: the bot plays a short clip from the server's music library, and whoever recognises it first presses the answer button.

  • Answer with a button and a small pop-up. The bot needs no permission to read messages, and you never type into the chat channel.
  • Fast means more points. The score drops with every second.
  • Hints cost points. Asking for a hint deducts from that question's score.
  • Streaks earn a bonus.
  • Four modes through the mode option of /quiz start: guess the song (default), the artist, the release year (exact is full points, within 2 years is partial, and it needs a year tag), or from a lyric line (the bot shows a line from the song's lyrics and plays the clip starting there when the lyrics are synced).
  • The server leaderboard is under /quiz top, with a period option: this month (default), last month or all time. Quiz seasons are calendar months. End a round with /quiz stop.

S-06

Stats and Wrapped

A drawing that sums up a year of listening
yours and the whole server's

sample image, illustrative figures

The bot remembers what you listen to and when, and turns it into fun numbers.

/mystats
Listening time, your most played tracks and artists, how often you hit skip, and your badges. Options: format (card, or a 9:16 1080x1920 story), theme (dark or light) and private (only you see it).
/leaderboard
The server leaderboard, plus the tracks the whole room loves most.
/wrapped
Your year in review as an image, drawn like a cyanotype blueprint, ready to show off in the server. It also has format (card, or a 9:16 1080x1920 story for Instagram and TikTok stories), theme and private.
/privacy

S-07

Lyrics that follow the singer

Karaoke style, the current line lights up
press 📜 on the Now Playing card

/lyrics or the 📜 button opens the lyrics of the current track, with the line being sung highlighted in real time, like karaoke.

Lyrics come from three places, in the order the bot looks:

  1. 1An .lrc file next to the music file, with the same name.
  2. 2Lyrics embedded in the tags of the music file itself.
  3. 3Online lookup (optional) from LRCLIB. This sends the title, artist and duration to that public service; the host can turn it off.

Track name on the voice channel. The bot writes a "Now playing: track name" line straight into the voice channel's status, so anyone scrolling the channel list can see it. Not a fan? Turn it off with /settings vc-status.

Free 24/7 mode. /247 on keeps the bot in the voice channel even when nobody is left, and it rejoins by itself after a restart. Turn it off with /247 off and the bot goes back to its old habit: if the channel empties or the queue ends, it leaves after 60 seconds.

Switch on the radio option and the bot plays the whole music folder non-stop, /nhaajt style, so the channel always has sound even if nobody adds a track.

S-09

The goods-in gate

Valid files go into storage, the bot owner stamps them
the bot downloads nothing from YouTube

/contribute (submit, pending, stats): you attach your own music file and tick a box confirming that you own it or are allowed to share it. The file goes into an approval queue, and the bot owner (not a server admin, because the music library is shared by every server) gets a direct message with Approve and Reject buttons. Once approved, the file moves into the music/Contributions/ folder, becomes searchable with /local, and you are notified by direct message.

  • Off by default. Needs CONTRIBUTIONS=on in .env, then /settings contributions enabled for each server.
  • Real audio files only, up to 30 MB (adjustable), between 10 seconds and 20 minutes long, and only Discord CDN links are accepted.
  • Duplicate and spam protection: duplicate files (compared by hash) are rejected, each person may have at most 3 files pending and 5 per day, and files waiting more than 14 days expire automatically.

/request (add, list, vote, mine, remove, done, dismiss): suggest a track by name or by a YouTube, Spotify or SoundCloud link. The bot downloads nothing and never opens that link; it only adds an entry to the wishlist along with votes. When a matching file shows up in storage (an approved contribution, or the owner dropping a file into music/), everyone who suggested or voted is notified automatically by direct message. Each person can have at most 5 open requests.

S-10

Playlists and server radio

Keep what is good
and let the radio carry on

/playlist comes in two kinds: personal playlists (yours, working on every server) and server playlists (shared; changing them needs the DJ role if one is set). A playlist holds both library files and online tracks, up to 25 playlists per owner and 200 tracks each.

/playlist create
Creates a new playlist.
/playlist save
Saves the current track plus the whole queue into a playlist.
/playlist add
Adds the current track to a playlist.
/playlist play
Plays a playlist, with an optional shuffle.
/playlist view, list
Shows the tracks in a playlist, or lists the playlists.
/playlist remove, delete
Removes one track from a playlist, or deletes the whole playlist.

/radio starts an endless server radio over the music library. Each round is ordered by the server's 👍/👎, the tracks the server plays through versus skips early (last 90 days), and what it usually plays around the current hour. With ANALYSIS=on the radio also leans a little toward a mood that fits the time of day: chill at night, steady in the morning, upbeat by day. The radio survives bot restarts.

/privacy delete removes your personal playlists. See also the Privacy page.

S-11

Companion bots

A few small bots to make a quiet channel less quiet
a separate program, entirely optional

This is a separate, optional program in the same repo (src/companions/), not part of the music bot. You give it 2 to 8 small bots, one Discord application and token each, all running in one process. Every so often one of them asks a question, tells a joke or a riddle, shares a fun fact, or chats with another bot. If nobody answers after a few minutes, another bot jumps in. If a person joins the conversation the bots step back, and anyone who replies gets a short thank-you. An empty channel is a channel problem; try not to make it yours.

  • Open about being bots. They keep Discord's BOT tag and have five personalities: Pip (the cheerful hype bot), Grumble (the sarcastic grump), Nova (the space and facts nerd), Sage (the deadpan philosopher) and Bean (the chaotic gremlin). They never pretend to be human, and they run on normal bot tokens only.
  • Content written into the repo. English and Vietnamese banks of questions, jokes and riddles, fun facts and bot-to-bot banter. Nothing is sent to any external service, and nothing repeats until the others have been used.
  • Good manners built in. One channel you choose; quiet hours (default 23:00 to 08:00 in the configured time zone); a daily limit (Calm up to 4 conversations a day, Normal 8, Lively 14); nothing while people are chatting; typing indicators; no pings.
  • They do not read message content. They only use the non-privileged Guilds and GuildMessages intents.

The commands live on the first companion bot and need the Manage Server permission. They belong to the companion bots and are not counted in the music bot's 38 slash commands.

/companions setup channel [language]
Choose the channel and language, and turn them on.
/companions on, off
Turn them on or off.
/companions frequency level
Calm (up to 4 a day), Normal (8) or Lively (14).
/companions quiet from until
The hours they stay silent.
/companions now
Start a conversation right away, to test it.
/companions status
Current settings and when the next chat is due.

Setup is on the Setup page. The honest part: the logic is covered by automated tests with a fake clock, but it has not been run against a live Discord server yet, so the timing and content will probably need tuning. Use it in servers you run, and tell your members what the bots are.
