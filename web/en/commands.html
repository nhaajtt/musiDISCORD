S-01

Command table

38 slash commands
type / in Discord to see suggestions

Parameters in parentheses are optional
CommandWhat it does
Playback
/play song name or link (source)Plays by song name or a YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify link; playlists work too. Pick the search source when you type a song name.
/search query (source)Searches by name and shows up to 10 results as numbered buttons, so you choose which one to play. You can pick the search source; the menu expires after 60 seconds.
/local querySearches the bot's music library by song, artist or album, with suggestions as you type.
/album album namePlays a whole album from the library, in track order.
/artist artist namePlays the songs of one artist from the library.
/nhaajtShuffles the entire music folder, avoids back-to-back songs by the same artist and favors liked songs; when a round ends it reshuffles and keeps going until /stop or /leave.
/radioAn endless server radio over the music library. Each round is ordered by the server's 👍/👎, the tracks it plays through versus skips early (last 90 days), what it usually plays around the current hour, and (with ANALYSIS=on) a small push toward a mood that fits the time of day: chill at night, steady in the morning, upbeat by day. It survives restarts.
Favorites and playlists
/favorites add, remove, list, playManages your own favorites list: add, remove, view, and play the whole list.
/playlist create, save, add, play (shuffle), view, remove, delete, listPersonal playlists (yours, working on every server) and server playlists (shared; changing them needs the DJ role if one is set). save stores the current track plus the queue, add adds the current track, and play can shuffle. They hold both local files and online tracks, up to 25 playlists per owner and 200 tracks each.
Controls
/pause, /resumePause and resume.
/skipSkips the current song; with a crowd listening, it goes to a vote.
/stopStops playback and clears the queue.
/leaveMakes the bot leave the voice channel.
/seek secondsJumps to a point in the song.
/volume levelVolume from 1 to 150.
/loop modeLoop off, repeat the current song, or repeat the whole queue.
/shuffleShuffles the queue.
/filter effect, speed, pitch, eq, status, reseteffect toggles an effect on or off (bass boost, nightcore, vaporwave, 8D, karaoke, tremolo, vibrato, mono), and effects stack. speed changes tempo from 0.5 to 2x with the pitch unchanged, pitch changes pitch from 0.5 to 2x with the speed unchanged, eq sets bass, mid and treble levels from -2 to 10. status shows what is applied and reset clears everything.
/remove positionRemoves one song from the queue.
/bumpStarts a vote to move a queued song to the front.
Info
/queueShows the queue as a new-style card, paged, with page buttons.
/nowplayingShows the Now Playing card with a live progress bar.
/lyricsSynced lyrics, with the line being sung highlighted.
/statsBot status: uptime, ping, server count, Lavalink, nodes up and shard count.
/helpThe command list, grouped.
Contributions and requests
/contribute submit, pending, statsSubmit your own music file (with a confirmation that you have the right to share it) for the bot owner to review, see pending files, and view stats. Off by default.
/request add, list, vote, mine, remove, done, dismissRequest a song by name or link, vote on other people's requests, and manage your own. The bot downloads nothing from links.
Smart library
/vibe moodMood radio (chill, easy, upbeat, intense) built from songs analyzed for tempo and energy. Off by default; needs ANALYSIS=on.
/similar (count)Queues the library songs most similar to the one playing.
/library status, run, stop, review, forgetFor the bot owner: check tagging and analysis progress, run or stop it, review suggested tags, and drop an automatic tag from a song.
Music quiz
/quiz start (mode), stop, top (period)Starts or stops a music quiz round drawn from the server's library, and shows the leaderboard. mode: guess the song (default), the artist, the release year (exact is full points, within 2 years is partial, needs a year tag) or from a lyric line (shows a line from the song's lyrics and plays the clip starting there when the lyrics are synced). period: this month (default), last month, all time. Quiz seasons are calendar months.
Stats and privacy
/mystats (format, theme, private)Your listening stats: time, top songs, top artists, skips, badges. Pick a card or a 9:16 story (1080x1920) for Instagram and TikTok stories, a dark or light theme, and private so only you see it.
/leaderboardThe server leaderboard and the most-liked songs.
/wrapped (format, theme, private)A year-in-review image, drawn like a cyanotype blueprint. Pick a card or a 9:16 story (1080x1920) for Instagram and TikTok stories, a dark or light theme, and private so only you see it.
/privacy stats, deleteStops recording stats about you, or deletes your data (personal playlists included).
Administration
/settings view, dj-role, volume, fair-queue, vc-status, contributionsView and change server settings: DJ role, default volume, fair queue, song name on the voice channel, and accepting contributions. Requires the Manage Server permission.
/247 on, off (radio)Turns 24/7 mode (staying in the voice channel) on or off; the radio option plays the whole folder nonstop.
S-02

Schedule of items

The small things that keep the bot
from annoying the whole server

Each item has a mark, like the door schedule in a house plan
MarkItemCommandHow it works
D-01DJ role/settings dj-roleOnly this role (and people with Manage Server) can control the music. Leave it empty and anyone can.
D-02Default volume/settings volumeFrom 1 to 150, applied each time the bot joins a voice channel.
D-03Skip votethe ⏭ button or /skipWith three or more listeners, the song only changes once half of them agree. The person who requested the song and the DJ skip instantly.
D-04Fair queue/settings fair-queueEveryone gets a turn in rotation, so nobody takes over the whole queue.
D-05Jump the queue/bumpA vote to move a queued song up to play next.
D-06Song name on the voice channel/settings vc-statusThe bot writes "Now playing: ..." into the voice channel status. On or off per server.
D-0724/7 mode/247The bot stays in the channel even when it is empty and rejoins after a restart. A radio option plays the whole folder.
D-08Queue memoryautomaticWhen the bot restarts, the queue is reloaded so you can keep listening without re-adding songs.
D-09Auto leaveautomaticWhen the channel empties or the songs run out, the bot leaves after 60 seconds so it does not hog the spot. With /247 on, it stays.
D-10Privacy/privacyEveryone can stop stats being recorded about them or delete their own data, personal playlists included.
D-11File contributions/contribute, /settings contributionsOff by default. The bot owner reviews every file before it enters the library.
D-12Wish list/requestRecords a song name with votes and downloads nothing. If a matching file turns up, the requester is notified.
D-13Auto tagging/libraryOff by default. Identifies untagged songs by audio fingerprint (AcoustID, MusicBrainz) and fills in title, artist, album and cover. Tags are stored separately and the original music files are never modified; low-confidence matches go to the bot owner for manual review.
D-14Audio analysis/vibe, /similarOff by default. Runs in the background on your own machine: estimates each song's tempo, energy and brightness to power mood radio or find similar songs.
D-15Several bots at oncedocker compose --profile multiEach bot has its own token but shares Lavalink and the music folder; tags and audio features are shared, stats data is separate.
D-16Status displaythe /display pageOff by default. A 480×320 page showing the current song, with touch buttons; suits a 3.5 inch TFT screen on a Raspberry Pi or a phone on your home network. Controls need a token.
D-17Backup, auto update, monitoringscripts in scripts/Periodic data backups, auto updates from GitHub that roll back if the bot is unhealthy, and Uptime Kuma watching over it.
D-18Help and status/help, /statsA grouped command list; ping, server count, Lavalink RAM, nodes and shards.
D-19Several Lavalink nodesLAVALINK_NODES, docker compose --profile haList several nodes, for example main=lavalink:2333,backup=lavalink2:2333. When one node goes down its players move to another. Every node must see the same music folder.
D-20ShardingSHARDSOptional: auto or a number; all shards run in one process. One shard is enough up to 2,500 servers.
